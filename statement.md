## Problem Statement
  Managing personal finances manually makes tracking expenses difficult, Understand Spending patterns, and track financial activities.

## Scope
  Designed to help user record, manage and analyze their personal expenses

## Target User
  1. Users who want to track financial expenses
  2. Users with vast financial expenses which can't be tracked manually
  3. Users who want to control their expenditures and save money

## High Level Features
  1. Categorize expenses
  2. Analyze spending
  3. Manage expenses
